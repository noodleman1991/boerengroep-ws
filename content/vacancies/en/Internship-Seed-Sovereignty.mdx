description: >
  We are excited to announce a new internship opportunity that awaits you! 


  Are you passionate about agroecology and community-driven initiatives? Do you
  thrive in dynamic environments where you can make a tangible impact? If so, we
  are currently looking for someone to work on seed sovereignty with us!


  This internship will consist of two parts:


  * Help organize Reclaim the Seeds Wageningen.Reclaim the Seeds is an annual
  festival in different places in the Netherlands and Boerengroep will again
  host the Wageningen version of this interesting and impactful gathering around
  seed sovereignty. 

  * Next to the organization of this festival, there will be space to work on
  another project regarding seed sovereignty, as a big seed nursery just got
  bankrupt. You will look into opportunities to help navigate seed distribution
  over Dutch smallholder farmers.


  **Project tasks:**


  * Structuring the organization of Reclaim the Seeds. Participate and prepare
  meetings with volunteers (together with the Boerengroep coördinator). 

  * Organizing the practical aspects of the program: food, space, costs,
  registrations, etc.

  * Promotion 

  * Working together and coördinating a group of volunteers (together with the
  Boerengroep coördinator)

  * Involvement in the whole Reclaim the Seeds festival

  * Finding sponsorships

  * Navigate in the Dutch landscape of seedling offerings and requests in order
  to help find a way of distributing seedlings to smallholder farmers. 


  **What we offer**


  * An opportunity to expand your network of farmers, professors, organisations
  and more. 

  * An office in Wageningen

  * Flexible work times

  * Hands-on experience in farmer-led initiatives

  * Lots of fun with our small Boerengroep team :) 


  **How to Apply:**


  If you are enthusiastic about diving into seed sovereignty, help organize this
  inspiring festival and work with farmer led initiatives, please submit your
  resume, and a cover letter outlining your relevant experience and vision for
  the project, to st.boerengroep\@wur.nl.


  Application Deadline: January 7

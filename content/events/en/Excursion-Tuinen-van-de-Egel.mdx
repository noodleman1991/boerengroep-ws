  Join our excursion to Tuinen van de Egel! This market garden, between Zutphen
  and Deventer is communally owned by Stichting Kapitaloceen, which buys land
  free from the market with the ideal to use it for humans and non-humans. Find
  more information on their website: tuinenvandeegel.nl


  Right now the farmers need our help for their winter works! They're looking
  for as many hands as possible because there are endless tasks to help with.
  With Boerengroep we want to join their working days to meet them, meet other
  volunteers, get to know the farm and the farmers and most important do some
  work!


  Let's get active and join us to the farm! Bring farm proof clothes and gloves
  if you have them. Snacks are appreciated, soup will be served by the farmers
  during lunch.

title: 'Critical Perspectives at WUR - Meet, Greet & Chill'
description: >-
  Are you curious what radical organizations/initiatives live in and around
  Wageningen?


  Join us on Wednesday 10th of September, from 19:00 at the Clockhouse Building,
  old library room upstairs, where three critical organizations are joining
  forces for the event ''Critical Perspectives at WUR: Meet& greet". 

  This event aims at bringing together the nodes of the web of radical
  initiatives, organizations and programs that bloom in Wageningen. We're hoping
  with this evening to inform, inspire and motivate new and old students, staff
  and active participants of Wageningen life. 

  Join with:

  - Support groups and communities: BIPOC collective Wageningen, FemSex, Purple
  Group

  - Foundations: Boerengroep & Inspringtheater, OtherWise, RUW & Jester

  - Activist groups: Wageningen for Palestine, Scientist Rebellion

  - WUR teachers: Mark Vicol (RSO), Jennifer Veilleux (GEO)

  - and... your initiative?  


  We welcome anyone joining to contribute with a small pitch and start a
  discussion on a topic you’re involved with.


  Expect a relaxed evening with chats, snacks, and nice people!


  For any questions, write us at otherwise@wur.nl


  Hope to see you there!

  Boerengroep & Inspringtheater, Otherwise, RUW & Jester.

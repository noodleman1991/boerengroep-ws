The FEI 2026 is now over, and once again, it was an amazing eye-opening experience for everyone!! This year, we had fifteen participants, from very various backgrounds and various nationalities, and we had so nice and enriching discussions all together, and also a lot of fun! We learned a lot from all different speakers, we had some lecturers from university, from organizations, and a lot of farmers. That was quite complementary and gave us a good overview on a large range of aspects of agroecology, through very various ways of learning: lectures, interactive workshops, discussions, but also excursions and even a Theater of the Oppressed session… We learned about sociological research on agriculture, feminism and the place of women in agriculture, the decolonial aspects of globalized agriculture, but also the actions of La Via Campesina worldwide, and food sovereignty. Several excursions to different farms in the surroundings, such as Ommuurde Tuin or Lichtveen, and nice discussions that took place there or with the farmers, allowed us to gain hands-on knowledge as well. We learned about farming year-round depending on the seasons, shared resources in agriculture, decision making and business models, tools in agroecology, soil health, and we experienced scything practices as well. The participants also had the opportunity to volunteer during two weeks at an agroecological farm, and they all came back enthusiastic and fulfilled (and also a little bit tired…) and with a lot of concrete knowledge about what it is to be a farmer and the challenges they face. Finally, we pretended to be apprentice radio host when recording our podcasts about agroecology at the studio, and we had so much fun! By the way, you can listen to them on our website farmexperienceinternship.wordpress.com, it worths checking out!! 

We concluded the FEI 2026 with an opening on the future and what to do with all this knowledge acquired, and also a workshop on how to become a farmer, in case the FEI gave some inspiring plans for future to our participants…😊 It was very interesting to hear all the participants reflect on their plans considering all the aspects of farming they have discovered during the FEI, especially social and political ones. Some of them want to find ways to help change the system, to put their degree or research to work for the agroecological transition, some want to bring this knowledge to their home countries, some others want to become farmers… So, now, all the participants are getting back to their lives, their studies, their routine, but with a slightly more open-minded and aware perspective on the realities of farming, and they are going to spread a little bit more this knowledge about agroecology. 

Thus, once again this year, we can say: mission accomplished!! The FEI managed to connect farmers to students, students to farmers, and the perspectives of this process are so exciting!!

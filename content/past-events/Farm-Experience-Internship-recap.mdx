One bad and one good news: Summer is over, but … also this year’s edition of the FEI was a great success!!!

Between the 14th of July and the 8th of August the 12th edition of the Farm Experience Internship took place and like usual, everything went perfectly. 13 participants discovered new insights about agroecology thanks to field visits, like to the Food Forest of Droevendaal, and farm visits, as for example to farmers part of the Streekwaar association and of other towns near Wageningen, and many more activities. During the first and the forth week of the FEI, we were based in the Creative Garden in Wageningen. External speakers, some academic some not, gave us interesting presentations about pesticide impact on human health, land access, La Via Campesina, Food Cooperations with Colombian peasants, feminism and indigenous knowledge, and many more. Some of these topics were new for our participants, which asked relevant questions and got inspired by agroecology even more. What is so special about the FEI is the active engagement of both organizers and participants. Our passionate volunteers also contributed to the program by giving workshops about the living soil, humanure, the history of mankind and agriculture through a walk in the woods of Wageningen, and intergenerational farming and the challenges of farmers thanks to a theatre play. One participant gave an eye opening presentation about bees and even brought honey for us to taste!

The delicious lunches provided by the catering business Radical Radishes and made by food donations by Odin, The Ommuurde Tuin, De Gieterij and De Hoge Born, were one of the peak moments of the day. We re-discovered “forgotten” vegetables and discovered new recipes. The lunches and evenings activities, like movie screenings and potlucks, strengthened the social ties that formed during the FEI.

During the second and the third week, all participants went working on their chosen farms. Some of them were on a vegetables farm, occupied planting, weeding, and harvesting. Others worked on a sheep farm, where they learned the arts of cheese and ice-cream making, and maybe also ate many of the latter :-p

All of 13 participants had a great experience on the farm, getting their hands dirty, working together and in contact with consumers, and having good nights sleeps after a long day under the sun or rain.

We can conclude this FEI season by confirming that once again our participants, organizers, external speakers and farmers were all very happy and satisfied, and that it was all thanks to them for making it happen!

Thank you and see you next year!

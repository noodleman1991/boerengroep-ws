      The Boerengroep Auditorium hosts larger public lectures, panel discussions, and events
      that bring together diverse perspectives on food systems, agriculture, and sustainability.


      These events are open to students, researchers, farmers, activists, and the broader
      public interested in learning about and engaging with critical questions about our
      food future.


      **Event Format**


      - Public lectures by prominent speakers

      - Panel discussions with multiple experts

      - Film screenings and discussions

      - Book launches and presentations

      - Larger community gatherings


      **Venue**


      Events are typically held at accessible locations in Wageningen and surrounding areas.
      Check individual event listings for specific venue information.

Welcome to the Open Pot: come to share a healthy vegan meal with us!! But it is not only about eating together: the Open Pot initiative aims to provide lunch to students at little to no charge, because food should not be a commodity, food is a commons, a universal right. Through volunteering, no kitchen rental costs, local and seasonal produce, we try to cut costs to make it possible. Check the Open Pot Student Kitchen section (in the Activities tab) for more info!!

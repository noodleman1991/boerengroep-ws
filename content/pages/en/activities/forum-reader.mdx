      The Forum Reader is a collaborative reading and discussion series where we explore
      critical texts on food systems, agriculture, political economy, and social movements.


      Through careful reading and collective discussion, we engage deeply with theoretical
      and analytical works that help us understand the challenges and possibilities for
      transforming our food systems.


      **How It Works**


      - Each session focuses on selected readings (articles, book chapters, reports)

      - Participants read the materials in advance

      - We meet to discuss, debate, and connect ideas to practice

      - Sessions are facilitated to ensure inclusive dialogue


      **Topics Covered**


      - Food sovereignty and agrarian movements

      - Political ecology and agriculture

      - Capitalism and food systems

      - Peasant studies and rural social movements

      - Food justice and equity

      - Environmental politics


      **Join the Discussion**


      Check our calendar for upcoming Forum Reader sessions. Readings are shared in advance
      with registered participants.

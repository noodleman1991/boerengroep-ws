      ### Nóri

      ##### Secretary

      Hi, I’m Nóri from Hungary, studying at WUR. I’m interested in policy, especially land policy and how it shapes agriculture.

      I enjoy learning languages and exploring cultures, especially how older ways of living still influence how we relate to the world and connect to the land.

      Reconnecting with the Earth and building a more mutual relationship with nature feels like a real way forward today, especially through being outside and doing things.

      That’s what I like about Boerengroep, learning by doing, staying curious, and not being afraid to get your hands dirty.

      ### Camille

      ##### FEI Intern

      Hi! I’m Camille, from France, I study Sustainable Production, Supply Chain and Territories there. I’m constantly trying to understand, learn and think about how to build a fair and local agri-food system that leaves no one behind, that pays its farmers fairly, without destroying natural ecosystems. I gradually discovered agroecology, and now there’s no turning back : I'm passionate about it !

      I’m really excited about meeting inspiring and passionate people, with so many different backgrounds, because I do think we all become richer by talking together.

      My internship at Boerengroep allows me to work on all those things I’m passionate about, and broaden my horizon.

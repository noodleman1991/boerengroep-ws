      # Boerengroep Privacy Policy Declaration


      This privacy policy applies to personal data that will be processed by
      Stichting Boerengroep. In this privacy declaration we provide insight in
      the way we gather personal data, which data we gather and for which
      reasons.


      We respect your privacy and ensure that the personal information you
      provide is treated confidentially. We will only use your data for the
      purposes for which it was obtained.


      **Data Collection**


      We collect personal data in the following ways: via Google Forms for
      activities or to be engaged with the organization and via email. We will
      at any time only ask for the information from you that is necessary. In
      some situations it can be necessary that we collect more information (e.g.
      dietary preference, car license, bike available), in that case we will
      always explain why we ask this information and it won’t be obligatory for
      you to fill it in.


      **Correspondence**


      When you send us an email or text message, we keep this email or message
      to ensure the best possible correspondence. The mail data is stored on the
      server of Wageningen University, and whatsapp messages via Meta. We will
      not communicate any information to third parties without your permission.


      **Expiry date**


      Personal data collected through registration or subscription forms (Google
      Forms) will be saved for a maximum one year. As stated above, information
      in regard to correspondence is saved for a longer time.


      **Amendments**


      You can unsubscribe from a mailing list or have your data deleted or
      updated from our files at any time. Send an email with your request to
      st.boerengroep\@wur.nl attn. privacy.


      For any personal data that you like to change in our database, email
      st.boerengroep\@wur.nl attn. privacy as well.


      **Pictures**


      Pictures that are taken during events or activities of Boerengroep can be
      published on social media or the website of Boerengroep. If you do not
      want pictures of yourself to be published or only under certain
      circumstances, make it known to the organization.


      If we want to use pictures of you for other purposes, we will first ask
      your permission. You will not be consulted when you are in the background
      of a picture.


      **Website**


      The website of Stichting Boerengroep is hosted by VDX, for their privacy
      regulations see:  https\://www\.vdx.nl/privacy-statement/ 


      **Adjustments to this privacy policy**


      This privacy policy is tailored to the use and possibilities of this
      website and to the current activities of our organization. If the use of
      the website and/or the scope of the activities change, this privacy policy
      may also change. It is therefore advisable to consult this privacy
      statement regularly and check for changes.


      **Questions and feedback**


      If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact our
      board functionary privacy by email: st.boerengroep\@wur.nl.

  *Volunteers needed for the StreekWaar market stall!*

  StreekWaar is looking for volunteers to help out at the market stall on
  Saturdays in Wageningen. Are you into local and sustainable food? Do you want
  to get involved with our local food community? Are you proactive, welcoming
  and friendly?

  Then we are looking for you! 


  What does it involve?

  - half-day shifts on Saturdays as often as suits you (7h30-12h30 or
  12h30-17h30)

  - helping setting up/tidying up the products on the stall

  - helping selling products from various StreekWaar members

  - small volunteer compensation, plus any leftover veggies


  You'll always be with an experienced StreekWaar producer who will be there to
  supervise/ show you what to do.

  Ideally you are able to help on a regular basis throughout the autumn and
  winter.  Speaking Dutch is not a requirement. We do value a social personality
  and good communication skills. Interested? Contact Pauline via
  info@streekwaar.nl.


  NB: StreekWaar is a local network of food entrepreneurs with a focus on
  (agro)ecological food. Among others, the members share a stall at the
  Wageningen market, with a diverse assortment of local products for your weekly
  groceries.

  More about StreekWaar: streekwaar.nl

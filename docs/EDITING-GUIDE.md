# How to edit the site

This guide is for the people who keep the Boerengroep and Inspringtheater websites up to date.
You do not need to know anything technical. If you can write an email and attach a photo, you
can do everything here.

Three things make it easy:

- Every box you can fill in has a short explanation right under it.
- Nothing you do can break the site. The worst case is a page that looks odd, and you can
  always go back to how it was (see "I made a mistake").
- What you change shows on the website within a few seconds.

## Log in

1. Go to the website's address followed by `/admin`, for example `www.boerengroep.nl/admin`.
2. Type your email address and password.
3. Forgot your password? Click "Forgot password" and you get an email with a link to choose a
   new one.

There is one place to log in for both websites. If you work on both, the first screen asks
which website you want to work on: Boerengroep or Inspringtheater. Boerengroep is chosen to
begin with. Click the other name to switch. Everything you then see and change belongs to that
website only.

**If a list looks empty**, check which website is chosen. Each website has its own pages,
events, news and pictures. Inspringtheater has no news yet, for example, so its list of news
is empty while Boerengroep's is not.

The first screen has three parts:

1. Big shortcuts for the things people do most, such as "Add an event".
2. "How do I…?": short answers to the questions people ask most. Click a question to open it.
3. "Everything on this website": every kind of content, what it is, and how many there are.

The menu on the left (the button with three lines, at the top left) has the same things:

| In the menu | What you find there |
| --- | --- |
| Pages | All pages of the website |
| Calendar | Events, the kinds of events, and stories of past events |
| News and vacancies | News items and vacancies |
| Library | All pictures and files, and the people named at events and news |
| Forms | The forms you made, and what people filled in |
| Site settings | Logo, menu, footer, newsletter texts and calendar options |
| People and websites | Who can log in, and the two websites themselves. Only admins see this |

## Save, or publish?

Pages, news items and stories of past events have two buttons at the top right:

- **Save draft** keeps your work, but visitors do not see it yet. Use it while you are busy.
- **Publish changes** puts it on the website.

The button with the eye, next to them, shows the page as it will look, before you publish.

Events, vacancies, pictures and site settings have only **Save**. They are on the website as
soon as you save.

## Change a page

1. Click **Pages** in the menu and click the page you want.
2. A page is built from **blocks**: parts stacked from top to bottom, such as a headline with a
   picture, a text, a row of photos. Click a block to open it.
3. Change the text or the picture.
4. Click **Publish changes**.

To move a block, drag it by the six dots on its left. To remove one, click the three dots on
its right and choose Remove.

## Make a new page

1. Click **Pages**, then **Create New**.
2. Fill in the **Title**.
3. Fill in the **Address**: the last part of the web address, in lowercase and with hyphens
   for spaces. For a page called "Our history", type `our-history`.
4. If the page belongs under another page, choose that page at **Sits under**. "History" under
   "About us" then gets the web address `/about-us/history`.
5. Click **Add block** and choose what the page starts with. Most pages start with
   **Opening** (a headline) and continue with **Text**.
6. Click **Publish changes**.

A new page is not in the menu by itself. Add it there: see [Arrange the menu](#arrange-the-menu).

Changing the address of a page that people already know breaks their old links. If you must,
ask your admin to add a forwarding address (Site settings, Forwarding addresses).

### The blocks you can choose

Every block starts with one line that says what it is for. Every block also has a
**Background**: white, or one of five colours. A page reads best when white and coloured
blocks take turns.

For text and pictures:

| Block | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Opening | The top of a page: a headline, a line under it, buttons, and a picture or video |
| Text | Text with headings, lists, links, pictures, files, videos and photo galleries. You will use this one most |
| Picture with text | One photo with a few paragraphs beside it |
| Highlights | Three or four short points side by side |
| Numbers | A few figures that tell something, such as "50 years" |
| Quotes | What someone said about you |
| Announcement | One line in a coloured strip that leads somewhere, such as "Volunteers wanted" |
| Invitation | One clear next step with a button, usually at the bottom of a page |

For photos, video and files:

| Block | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Photo gallery | Photos, and videos if you like, that open large when clicked |
| Video | A YouTube or Vimeo video. It only starts loading when a visitor presses play |
| Downloads | Files people can download, each with its name and size |
| Podcast episodes | The newest episodes of the podcast, or ones you choose |

Blocks that fill themselves. You place them once and never have to touch them again:

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Events | The next events from the calendar |
| Latest news | The newest news items |
| Open positions | The vacancies people can apply for today |
| In the spotlight | One to three things you choose yourself |

See [What shows on the home page](#what-shows-on-the-home-page) for how to use these four.

For visitors to do something:

| Block | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Newsletter sign-up | The box where people leave their email address |
| Form | A form you made under Forms |
| Item for a donation | Something people get for a donation, such as a T-shirt. It is worded as a donation, not a sale |

## Pictures in a text

In a **Text** block you write as in any text program. To add a picture:

1. Click where the picture should come, on an empty line.
2. Type `/`. A small list appears. Choose **Upload**.
3. Choose a picture you uploaded before, or upload a new one.

The picture comes in as wide as the text. To change that:

1. Move the mouse over the picture. Three small buttons appear at its top right.
2. Click the first one, the **pencil**.
3. Choose a **Size**:
   - Small: a third of the text
   - Medium: half of the text
   - Large: three quarters of the text
   - As wide as the text
4. Choose a **Place**: left, in the middle, or right. With left or right, the text runs beside
   the picture. This works for small and medium pictures. A large picture always stands in the
   middle, because there is no room left to read beside it.
5. If you like, write a **Line under the picture**.
6. Click **Save changes**.

On a phone every picture gets its own line, whatever you chose. That happens by itself.

The second button on the picture (two arrows) swaps it for another picture. The third (a
cross) removes it from the text. The picture itself stays in the Library.

**Always describe the picture.** When you upload a picture, fill in "Describe the picture".
People who cannot see the picture hear this description read out.

## Photo galleries

A gallery shows several photos together. A click on a photo opens it large, and visitors can
leaf through the rest.

**Inside a text.** On an empty line in a Text block, type `/` and choose **Photo gallery**.
Drop all the photos in at once, and drag them into the order you want.

**As a part of the page.** Click **Add block** and choose **Photo gallery**. Here you can also
give the gallery a title, add videos, or show all photos of a past event.

In both you choose the **Size of the photos**:

- Mosaic: large and small photos mixed. Good for a lively overview.
- Small pictures, many in a row. Good for a lot of photos.
- Medium pictures.
- Large pictures, two in a row. Good for a handful of photos that deserve attention.

The words on a photo in a gallery are its **caption**. You write the caption on the photo
itself: open the photo under Library, Pictures and files.

## Files to download

You can upload PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, just like pictures.

**One file in a text**, such as "Year plan 2026":

1. In a Text block, write the words people should click, for example "Year plan 2026".
2. Select those words and click the **link** button (a chain) in the small bar that appears.
3. Choose **Internal Link**.
4. In the box under it, type part of the file's name, for example "year". Pick the file from
   the list. Files are listed under "Pictures and files".
5. Click **Save changes**.

Link to pages of your own site the same way: type part of the page's title and pick the page.
Such a link keeps working when the page or the file moves.

**A list of files**: add the block **Downloads** and choose the files. Each shows with its
name, kind and size.

## Add an event

1. Click **Add an event** on the first screen, or Calendar, Events, Create New.
2. Fill in the **Title** and when it **starts**. Times are Dutch time.
   - Leave "ends" empty if you do not know it.
   - Choose a date without a time for something that takes the whole day.
3. Choose the **Kind of event**, such as Talk or Excursion. The kind gives the event its colour
   on the calendar, and visitors can filter by it.
4. Type **where** it is, the way you would say it. A link to a map is made for you.
5. Add a **picture**: a poster or a photo.
6. If people need to sign up, write how under **How to sign up**. Leave it empty if people can
   just come.
7. Click **Save**. The event is on the calendar straight away, with its own page that people
   can share and add to their own calendar.

Later, if needed, change the **Status**:

- **Full** shows an orange label and hides the sign-up.
- **Cancelled** or **Postponed** keeps the event visible but crossed out, so people who planned
  to come find out.

Tick **Spotlight** to show an event first and larger on the home page.

**New kinds of events.** You make the kinds yourself: Calendar, Kinds of events. Give a kind a
name in English and in Dutch and one of eight colours. A kind appears in the filter on the
calendar as soon as one event uses it.

## Tell how an event went

1. Go to Calendar, **Stories of past events**, Create New.
2. Write how it went.
3. Drop all photos of the day into **Photos** at once.
4. If you have videos, add their YouTube or Vimeo links under **Videos**.
5. At **The event on the calendar**, choose the event. The event's page and the story then link
   to each other.
6. Click **Publish changes**.

## Write a news item

Your own news and news from friends are in one list: News and vacancies, **News**. Above the
list are three links to show all news, only your own, or only news from friends.

1. Go to News and vacancies, **News**, Create New.
2. Fill in the **Title** and the **Date**.
3. At **Kind of item**, choose "Article written here" if you write the text yourself, or "Link
   to another website" if you only want to point to something elsewhere. For a link, paste the
   address at **Link**.
4. At **Whose news**, choose your own organisation, or "News from friends" for news of others.
   News from friends shows on its own page.
5. Add a **Picture** and a **Short summary**. Both show wherever the item is listed.
6. A news item is written in one language. Choose it at **Language**, or leave it empty.
7. Click **Publish changes**.

The newest items show first everywhere, also on the home page. Tick **Put this item in the
spotlight** at the bottom to show one item first and larger there.

## Post a vacancy

1. Go to News and vacancies, **Vacancies**, Create New.
2. Fill in the **Title of the position** and choose the **Kind of position** (volunteer,
   internship, coordinator, board or other).
3. Say until when people can apply. There are two ways:
   - Tick **Always open, no deadline** for positions people can always apply for.
   - Or fill in **Apply until** with the last day.
4. Fill in what you know: what the work is, what you ask, what people get, who to contact.
   Boxes you leave empty simply do not show.
5. Click **Save**.

What happens with a last day:

- Up to and including that day, the vacancy says "Apply until" with the date.
- The day after, it says "Closed".
- Three days later it leaves the website by itself. You do not have to remove it.

A vacancy holds both languages in one. Write the English, switch the language at the top right
to Dutch, and write the Dutch. Until the Dutch is written, Dutch visitors read the English.

Tick **Put this vacancy in the spotlight** to show it first where a page lists the open
positions.

## What shows on the home page

The home page is a page like any other: find it under Pages, it is called Home. Four blocks
are made to keep it fresh without any work:

**Events** shows the next events from the calendar. Add an event, and it appears.

**Latest news** shows the newest news items. Write a news item, and it appears. In the block
you choose whose news (all, your own, or from friends) and how many items.

**Open positions** shows the vacancies people can apply for today. A vacancy appears when you
post it and disappears the day after its last day. If nothing is open, the block hides itself,
or says "nothing open right now" if you prefer that.

**In the spotlight** is for the one thing you want everybody to see this month. You choose it
yourself:

1. Open the Home page and click **Add block**, then **In the spotlight**.
2. At **What to show**, first choose the kind (a page, an event, a news item, a story of a
   past event, or a vacancy), then the one you mean.
3. That is enough. The title, a few lines of text and the picture are taken from the thing
   itself. If you want other words, another picture or other words on the button, fill them
   in right there.
4. Add a second and a third thing if you like. One thing shows large. Two or three show side
   by side.
5. Drag the block to where it should stand and click **Publish changes**.

When the month is over, open the block and choose something else, or remove the block.

All four blocks work on any page, not only on the home page.

## Arrange the menu

The menu is the row of links at the top of every page. Only site admins can change it.

1. Go to **Site settings** and click the tab **Menu**.
2. You see one row for every item of the menu, in the order of the website from left to right.
3. **To change the order**, drag a row up or down by the six dots on its left.
4. **To change an item**, click its row. It opens. Change the words people click on, and
   choose where it leads:
   - **A page**: pick a page of the website. The link keeps working when the page moves.
   - **A built-in section**: the calendar, the news, the vacancies and so on.
   - **Another address**: any web address, for example of another website.
5. **To add an item**, press "Add Menu item" under the last row.
6. **To remove an item**, press the three dots on the right of its row and choose Remove.
7. **To make a list that drops down** under an item, open the item and add links under "Links
   that drop down". Drag those to order them, just like the items.
8. Press **Save** at the top right. The website changes within seconds.

Do this once in English and once in Dutch for the words: switch the language at the top right
and fill in the Dutch words of each item. The order and the links are shared.

Tick **Show as a button** on the one item you most want visitors to click.

## Footer and other settings

Only site admins can change these. Go to **Site settings**. Next to Menu it has four more tabs:

- **General**: the name, the logo, the contact details and the social media links.
- **Footer**: the columns of links at the bottom of every page. It works like the menu: each
  column is a row, click it to open it, drag to change the order.
- **Newsletter**: the texts of the sign-up box, including the small print about what people
  agree to. At the top, "Does the sign-up reach Brevo?" tells you in plain words whether
  sign-ups arrive in your mailing list. See "The link with Brevo" below.
- **Calendar**: the text above the calendar, and whether it opens as a list or as a month.

### The link with Brevo

Brevo is the service that keeps your mailing list and sends the newsletter. The website adds
people to your list when they confirm their email address. For that it needs two things from
your Brevo account, and both go on the tab **Newsletter** of Site settings:

1. **The key.** In Brevo, click your name at the top right, then "SMTP & API", then "API
   keys", and make a new key. Copy it, paste it in the box **New Brevo key**, and press Save.
2. **The list number.** In Brevo, under Contacts, Lists, each list has a number in the column
   "ID". Fill it in at **Brevo list number** and press Save.

After saving, the check at the top of the tab tells you whether it works.

A key is like a password. That is why, once saved, it is never shown again: the box is empty
when you come back, and only the last four characters of the saved key are shown next to it so
you can tell which one it is. Leaving the box empty keeps the saved key. To replace it, paste a
new one. To stop using it, tick **Remove the saved Brevo key** and press Save.

## People and websites

Only admins see this part of the menu. It has two lists.

**People who can log in** is everyone with a login. To let a colleague in:

1. Press **Create New**.
2. Fill in their email address and a first password. Tell them the password yourself. They can
   change it after logging in.
3. Leave **Kind of account** on "Normal account".
4. Under **Websites this person works on**, press "Add Website", choose the website, and choose
   what they may do there:
   - **Editor**: write, change and publish.
   - **Admin of this website**: also change the menu, the footer and the settings, and add
     people.
5. Someone who works on both websites gets two rows, one per website.
6. Press **Save**.

To take someone's access away, open the person and delete them, or remove the row of the
website they should no longer work on.

**Websites** lists the two websites with their name and web address. You rarely need it. The
order of this list is the order of the two names on the first screen.

## Forms

1. Go to **Forms**, Create New.
2. Add the questions: a line of text, an email address, a number, a choice, a long answer or a
   tick box. Say which ones people must fill in.
3. Write the message people see after sending.
4. Put the form on a page with the block **Form**.

The answers arrive under Forms, Form responses.

**You also get every answer by email.** Each time someone fills in a form, or orders something
through an "Item for a donation", the website sends an email with everything they filled in to
your organisation's own address. Answer that email and you write straight to the person. Which
address it goes to is set under Site settings, General, at **Send form answers and orders
to**. Leave it empty and the emails go to the contact email address on the same tab.

To send an answer to more people, or to send the visitor a confirmation, add an email under
"Emails" on the form itself.

## Two languages

The website is in English and Dutch. At the top right of every screen in the admin is a switch
between the two.

- **Pages**: a page has an English and a Dutch version, each with its own title, address and
  blocks. Make the English one, switch to Dutch, and make the Dutch one. A page without a
  Dutch version shows the English one to Dutch visitors.
- **Vacancies and kinds of events**: one item holds both languages. Switch at the top right
  and fill in the other language.
- **Events, news and stories**: each is written in one language. Choose it at Language, or
  leave it empty to show it to everyone.
- **Menu, footer and other texts in Site settings**: fill them in once per language.

## I made a mistake

- **A page, news item or story**: open it and click **Versions** at the top. You see every
  earlier version with its date. Open the one you want back and click **Restore this
  version**.
- **Not sure how it will look?** Use Save draft and the eye button first. Publish when you are
  happy.
- **Removed a picture from a text by accident?** The picture is still in the Library. Add it
  again.
- **Something else?** Ask your site admin. Admins can also add people and help with a
  forgotten password.

The first screen after logging in has the same help in short, under "How do I…?".

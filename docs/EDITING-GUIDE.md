# Editing guide

Everything on the site is managed in the admin panel. This guide walks through it in the order
you will meet things. Every field in the admin panel also has a short explanation under it, so
you rarely need this page once you have read it.

## Logging in

Open `https://<site address>/admin` and log in with your email and password. "Forgot password"
on the login screen sends you a link to set a new one.

There is one admin panel for both sites, Boerengroep and Inspringtheater. If you work on both,
you see the two sites as tabs on the welcome screen and at the top of the menu. Choose one, and
everything you see and change belongs to that site. If you work on one site you see no tabs.

The welcome screen has shortcuts for the things people do most: add an event, change a page,
upload pictures, write a news item, tell how an event went, change the menu or footer, and read
form answers.

## What is live when

- **Pages, news and stories of past events** have drafts. "Save draft" keeps changes to yourself.
  "Publish" puts them on the site. The eye button at the top shows the page as it will look.
- **Events, vacancies, pictures and site settings** are live as soon as you save.
- A change shows on the site within seconds. There is no rebuild to wait for.

## The menu on the left

| Group | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| Pages | The pages of the site |
| Calendar | Events, and stories of past events |
| News and vacancies | News items, and vacancies |
| Library | Pictures and files, speakers and hosts, authors, tags |

## Pages

A page has a **title**, an **address** and **blocks**.

- **Address** is the last part of the web address, for example `history`. Use lowercase letters,
  numbers and hyphens. The home page uses `home`.
- **Sits under** makes a page a sub-page. `history` under `about-us` becomes `/about-us/history`.
- **Blocks** are the parts of the page from top to bottom. Press "Add block", pick one, fill it in.
  Drag a block by its handle to move it. Each block starts with one line that says what it is for.
- **Text** is the page's own plain text. It shows right under the opening block, before the
  other blocks.

A page that does not start with an Opening block gets its title as its heading. A page with
nothing on it lists the pages that sit under it.

Search engines and link previews show the page's title, and as its description the line under
the headline of the Opening block, or else the first paragraph of the page. Write those two with
a stranger in mind.

### Two languages

The language switch is at the top right of the edit screen. Title, address and blocks exist per
language, so the Dutch page can have its own address and its own blocks. A page without a Dutch
version shows the English one to Dutch visitors.

### When an address changes

Pages underneath move along. Old links would break, so add a forwarding address: Site settings,
Forwarding addresses, old address and new address. Only site admins can do this.

## The blocks

Every block has a **Background** choice: white, soft green, green, orange, blue or dark
green. Alternate white with a colour to give a page rhythm.

| Block | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Opening | The top of a page: headline, a line under it, buttons, a picture or a video |
| Events | The next events, events of one kind, or events you pick. Shows the first one large |
| Announcement | One line that links somewhere |
| Text | Running text with headings, lists, links, pictures, files and videos |
| Picture with text | A photo beside or above a few paragraphs |
| Highlights | Three or four short items of equal weight |
| Numbers | A few figures that say something |
| Quotes | What people say about you |
| Invitation | One clear next step with a button, usually at the end of a page |
| Video | A YouTube or Vimeo link. It only loads after a visitor presses play |
| Photo gallery | Photos and videos as a mosaic that open large, with captions. Your own choice, or those of a past event |
| Downloads | Files to download, with name, kind and size |
| Podcast episodes | The latest episodes, or ones you choose by a few words of their title |
| Newsletter sign-up | The sign-up box anywhere on a page |
| Form | A form you built under Forms |
| Item for a donation | Something people get for a donation, such as a T-shirt: pictures, details, the suggested donation, one button and small print you can edit. It is a donation, not a sale |

**Symbols.** Where a block asks for a symbol, type one of these names: Apple, ArrowRight, Bike,
BookOpen, Calendar, Camera, Clock, Download, ExternalLink, Film, Globe, GraduationCap, HandHeart,
Heart, Home, Info, Leaf, Mail, MapPin, Megaphone, Mic, Music, Newspaper, Phone, Podcast, Soup,
Sprout, Tractor, Users, Wheat.

## Events

Each event gets its own page that people can share and add to their own calendar.

- **Title, starts, ends.** Times are Dutch time. Leave "ends" empty if you do not know it. Pick a
  date without a time for a whole-day event. Two dates without times make a multi-day event.
- **Address** is made for you from the title and the date. Do not change it after sharing the link.
- **Status.** Going ahead, Full, Cancelled or Postponed. "Full" shows an orange badge and hides
  the sign-up. Cancelled and postponed events stay visible, crossed out, so people who saved them
  find out. The note next to it is for a short line such as "Waiting list: mail us".
- **Kind of event** sets the colour on the calendar and lets visitors filter.
- **Where.** Type the place as you would say it. A map link is made from it. Add an online
  meeting link for online or hybrid events.
- **Picture.** A poster or a photo. After uploading you can crop it and choose the point that
  must stay visible when the picture is cut for cards and share previews.
- **How to sign up.** Type what people should click, select it, and add the link to your sign-up
  form. Leave it empty if people can just come.
- **Spotlight** puts the event first and larger on the home page.

Visitors can subscribe to the whole calendar with one button. New events, changes and
cancellations then appear in their own Google, Apple or Outlook calendar. You can switch this
off under Site settings, Calendar.

## Stories of past events

Write how it went, and drop all photos of the day in **Photos** at once. They show as a mosaic.
Add **Videos** by their YouTube or Vimeo link, each with a caption. Videos get the large places
in the mosaic. Choose **The event on the calendar** and the event page links to the story and
back. A photo's caption is set on the photo itself, under Library, Pictures and files.

## News and vacancies

A news item is an article you write here or a link to another website. "News from friends"
shows on its own page. A vacancy shows on the Vacancies page under its kind. Attach a job
description as a file and it becomes a download.

These are written in one language. Set **Language** to English or Dutch, or leave it empty to
show the item in both.

## Pictures and files

Upload under Library, or straight from any picture field. Fill in **Describe the picture**
for people who cannot see it, and a **caption** if you want words on it in galleries. You can
upload PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, text and CSV files as well.

## Forms

Under Forms you build a form: add fields (text, email, number, choice, long text, tick box),
say which ones are needed, and write the message people see afterwards. Place it on a page with
the Form block or the "Item for a donation" block. Answers arrive under Form responses and can only be read by
people who work on your site. To get an email for each answer, add one under "Emails" on the form.

## Site settings

One screen with five tabs. Only site admins can change it.

- **General.** Name, logo, a logo for dark backgrounds (used in the footer), one-line
  introduction, contact details and social media.
- **Menu.** The items at the top of every page. Each item goes to a page of the site, a built-in
  section such as the calendar, or any other address. A link to a page keeps working when that
  page moves. An item can have a dropdown. Tick "Show as a button" for the one thing you most
  want visitors to do.
- **Footer.** Link columns and the small links at the very bottom.
- **Newsletter.** The texts of the sign-up box, including the small print about consent, the
  thank-you people read after signing up, and the message after they confirm. At the top is a check, "Does the sign-up reach Brevo?". It
  says in plain words whether the link works, lists your Brevo lists with their numbers, and
  offers a button that adds everyone who is missing from the list.
- **Calendar.** The text above the calendar, the first view (list or month) and the subscribe
  button.

Fill in labels and texts once per language with the language switch at the top right.

## Who to ask

Your site admin can add people and reset access. Admins of a site can add editors for that site.

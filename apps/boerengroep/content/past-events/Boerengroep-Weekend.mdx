One bad and one good news: the weekend at Zonnegoed farm is already over, but… Boerengroep’s exploration turned out to be an unforgettable ecofarming adventure!

From the 20th to the 21th of September, around twenty participants (old and new members of Boerengroep) set off with tents, sleeping bags and rain boots to visit Zonnegoed V.O.F., run by Joost van Strien. His 90-hectare “striped paradise” is a vegan farm managed with incredible care: no plowing, no animal manure, just compost and patience. Thanks to this method, nine-meter-wide crop rotation strips flourish with towering plants, buzzing pollinators, countless worms and soaring birds of prey.

The weekend started with a shared picnic on the first day, followed by hands-on workshops centered on ecofarming. Rain or shine, participants put on boots and waterproofs to weed the fields, later returning to the base camp for an inspiring theatre workshop. Through roleplay, we stepped into the shoes of farmers, biotech industries, consumers, supermarkets, and governments, and discovered many perspectives on agriculture.

After the physical and mental effort, a hearty vegetarian goulash brought everyone together around the table, with long evening conversations in front of the fire before a windy night in the tents. The next morning began with stacks of pancakes, fueling us for another round of workshops on humanure and soil. After a quick lunch, we headed back into the fields for more weeding before the bittersweet moment of packing up our tents.

Leaving Joost’s farm was not easy: it felt like leaving a piece of paradise. Yet, everyone went home with new knowledge, inspiration, and a strengthened community spirit. Thank you Joost, thank you Boerengroep! what a fantastic experience! Until the next weekend!
